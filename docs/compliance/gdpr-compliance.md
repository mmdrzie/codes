## Data Breach Management

### Breach Response Procedure

| Step | Timeline | Responsible Party | Evidence |
|------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| Detection and assessment | Immediately | Security team | Incident detection system |
| Containment | Within 2 hours | Operations team | Containment procedures |
| Notification to supervisory authority | Within 72 hours | DPO | Breach notification form |
| Communication to data subjects | Without undue delay | Communications team | User notification system |
| Documentation | Ongoing | All teams | Incident record |

### Breach Categories

| Category | Definition | Threshold | Response |
|----------|------------|-----------|----------|
| High risk | Likely to affect rights and freedoms | Risk assessment > 6 | Individual notification |
| Medium risk | Potential impact | Risk assessment 3-6 | Enhanced monitoring |
| Low risk | Minimal impact | Risk assessment < 3 | Documentation only |
